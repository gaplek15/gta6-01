Teluk Cahaya — an original 3D open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty. Raka, an ojek rider, takes three jobs from the fixer Darma across a sun-soaked tropical coastal city: deliver a package to the pier, steal the red Komet Merah sports car for Darma's garage, and escape a police crackdown to the safehouse warung. Explore the city on foot and by car, steal vehicles, outrun the police under a 1–5 star wanted level, fight back, drive taxi fares, collect golden coins, and save your progress. Everything — the city, characters, vehicles, names, story, art and sound — is original.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
